Built for West AfricaLambert × Phoenix Energy

Lambert Electromec designs, builds, and operates renewable power solutions — from solar photovoltaic systems to energy storage and waste-to-energy plants — engineered to cut costs, reduce emissions, and keep power flowing when the grid can't. Backed by our partnership with Phoenix Energy, part of the INDEVCO Group with more than 60 years of heritage, we bring proven, bankable renewable technology to homes, businesses, and industry across Nigeria and West Africa.

Energy reliability remains one of the biggest costs of doing business in West Africa. Our renewable energy division combines Lambert Electromec's local EPC capability with Phoenix Energy's international renewable project experience to deliver practical, bankable power infrastructure.

Together, we cover the full project lifecycle from feasibility and site assessment through engineering, supply, construction, installation, operation, and long-term maintenance.

Our Renewable Energy Solutions

Our Renewable Energy Solutions

01

Solar Photovoltaic & Solar Thermal

We design, install, and maintain high-quality solar systems and inverter backup solutions that are efficient, cost-effective, and built around each customer's power profile — available as:

  • On-Grid Solar PV Systems
  • Off-Grid Solar PV Systems
  • Hybrid Grid Solar PV Systems
02

Energy Storage Systems

Our Electrical Energy Storage Systems (EESS) keep renewable power available even when the sun isn't shining or the grid goes down. Paired with a solar PV installation, EESS improves supply reliability, supports fast frequency response and demand management, and reduces overall power quality issues — suited to grid-connected, commercial, and industrial off-grid demand.

03

Biomass & Waste-to-Energy

We convert organic and industrial waste streams into usable power, reducing disposal costs while generating reliable energy for the sites that produce that waste.

04

Wind, Landfill Gas & Waste Heat Recovery

Beyond solar, our capabilities extend to wind power, landfill gas capture, and waste heat and waste gas recovery — giving industrial clients more ways to turn energy that would otherwise be lost into usable power.

05

Energy Audit & Solar Feasibility Assessment

Before any equipment is specified, our energy consultants assess whether renewable energy is the right fit — reviewing energy production and consumption to eliminate waste, sizing the right solution for your site, and modelling the return on investment and payback period.
