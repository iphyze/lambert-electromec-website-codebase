Who We Are

Profile

Lambert Electromec Ltd is a leading Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) company with an international presence and expertise in Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing (MEP), particularly across West Africa.

Lambert Electromec is an EPC contractor that specializes in executing complex infrastructural projects that require the highest level of technical know-how in Buildings & Factories, Oil & Gas and Modular Refineries, Power Solutions (generation, renewable energy, distribution and energy management), Data Centres, and Facility Management.

Our History

Our History

Lambert Somec Nig. Ltd. was created in 2001 in affiliation with Lambert Somec Inc., itself founded in 1954 in Québec, Canada. Before becoming operationally independent from its partner in 2004, Canadian-based Lambert Somec Inc. passed on its heritage of first-class professionalism, top engineers and its exceptional overall operational system. In 2007, it changed its name to Lambert Electromec Ltd. and continued operating independently in the West African region.

Vision Statement

Our vision is to be the No. 1 electromechanical engineering firm and a pioneer of new technologies in Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC).

We pursue this vision through high-quality electromechanical installations, construction and reliable power solutions, while continually introducing innovative systems and knowledge.

Mission Statement

Mission Statement

Our mission is to build the future of comfort and safety in West Africa by providing a wide range of high-quality and innovative solutions.

To provide expertise through global investment in human capital, assets and divisions, along with our extensive network of partners. To increasingly develop our Local Content resources in the Oil & Gas and Construction markets and contribute to the development of local expertise and education.
