Who We Are

About Us

Lambert Electromec Ltd is a leading Engineering, Procurement and Construction (EPC) company with an international presence and expertise in Mechanical, Electrical and Plumbing (MEP), particularly across West Africa.

It is an EPC contractor that specializes in executing complex infrastructural projects that require the highest level of technical know-how in Oil & Gas and Modular Refineries, Power Generation and Distribution, Data Centre & Telecom Infrastructure, and Buildings & Factories.

History

From Lambert Somec heritage to independent West African delivery

Lambert Electromec Limited was created in affiliation with Lambert Somec Inc., itself founded in 1954 in Québec, Canada. Lambert Somec Inc. passed on its heritage of first-class professionalism, top engineers, and its exceptional overall operational system to Lambert Electromec Ltd., which operates independently in the West African region.
