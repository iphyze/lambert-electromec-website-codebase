Oil & Gas and Processing Solutions

Oil & Gas and Processing Solutions

Lambert Electromec offers extensive capabilities to support the Oil & Gas industry across the full project lifecycle. From early-stage project identification and pre-development planning to governance, engineering, commissioning, and final project handover, Lambert delivers end-to-end solutions that align with industry standards and client expectations.

Lambert Electromec has specialised capabilities to offer a range of processing solutions for the Oil & Gas industry. In collaboration with its technical partner, VFuels LLC, Lambert has successfully delivered various modular-based projects. These solutions can range from 1,000 to 30,000 barrels per day (bpd) per module for condensate and crude processing, in addition to gas processing units ranging from 1 million to 20 million standard cubic feet per day (mmscfd).
