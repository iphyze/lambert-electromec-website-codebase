Safety governance

Safety embedded in every operation

Keeping the environment healthy and safe wherever we work is a core value of Lambert worldwide. Since our operations in Nigeria began in 2001, Lambert has kept to this ideal through sound HSE practices and a strict adherence to national and local regulations. In all our locations and sites, HSE staff work alongside other personnel to ensure that safety rules are observed. The HSE Department has developed an incentive program to reinforce good safety performance and it is continually reviewed to ensure its effectiveness.

0105

Tree Transplanting Project – Sustainability Initiative | Tamale - Ghana

Lambert Electromec proudly supports the Tree Transplantation Project at the Tamale Old Airfield in Ghana—an initiative led by [Applied] Foreign Affairs, Institute of Architecture at the University of Applied Arts Vienna. As part of this sustainability-driven effort, mature Savannah trees were carefully relocated to establish a climate-resilient green corridor along the former runway.

Our contribution included vital infrastructure and logistical support, enabling the transformation of the Tamale Old Airfield into a shaded, multifunctional urban park.
